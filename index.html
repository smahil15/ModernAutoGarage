Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get an oil change?

Oil change intervals depend on your vehicle and the type of oil used. Check your owner's manual or contact us for a recommendation based on your vehicle.

How do I know if my brakes need to be checked?

Common signs include squeaking or grinding noises, vibration while braking, or a longer stopping distance. If you notice any of these signs, your brakes should be inspected.

Do I need an appointment for service?

Scheduling an appointment is recommended so we can prepare for your vehicle and service needs. Call us to schedule a convenient time.

What types of vehicles do you service?

Modern Auto Garage provides maintenance and repair services for many makes and models.

How long does a vehicle inspection take?

The time needed can vary depending on the vehicle and its condition. Contact us for more information when scheduling your inspection.
